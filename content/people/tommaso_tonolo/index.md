I am a postdoctoral researcher at the University of Padova, where I joined the LIPh Lab in September 2026.

I obtained my Bachelor's and Master's degrees in Physics from the University of Padova, spending part of my Master's studies at KU Leuven in Belgium. I then completed my PhD in Mathematics in Natural, Social and Life Sciences at the Gran Sasso Science Institute (GSSI) in L'Aquila in 2026.

During my PhD, I worked on the statistical mechanics of disordered systems, with a particular focus on complex ecological communities. I studied how network structure and heterogeneity of interactions influence their stability, collective behaviour and equilibrium properties.

My research interests lie in statistical physics and complex systems, particularly in the application of statistical mechanics to theoretical ecology and biological systems. I am interested in understanding how disorder, interactions and stochastic processes give rise to collective behaviour, scaling laws, and emergent properties, from ecological communities to broader biological phenomena.
