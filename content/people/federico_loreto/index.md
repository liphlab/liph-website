My name is Federico Loreto and I am a PhD student in Physics at the University of Padua.

I obtained my Bachelor’s degree in Physics from “La Sapienza” University of Rome with a thesis on the role of bias in Machine Learning. Afterwards, I earned a Master’s degree in the Physics of Biosystems curriculum offered by “La Sapienza”, graduating with a thesis on the Generalized Lotka-Volterra model with stochastic interactions under the supervision of Prof. Samir Suweis of the University of Padua.

I am particularly interested in the computational side of ecological modeling, and my current work focuses on bridging the gap between empirical data and mathematical models such as Lotka-Volterra and Consumer-Resource. Something that fascinates me is the introduction of time-dependent interactions and the study of ecological models as frequency filters able to transform the input signals, employing information theory as a tool to guide empirical procedures.
